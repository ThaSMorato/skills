> Call the Skill tool with `asking` before you report or ask: resolve every id to what it means, offer a structured choice where the answer is a closed set, and ask only what is genuinely a decision.

Preflight: what the screen is for is written somewhere (a PRD, an FDD, a ticket, or the owner's request); with none, run the brief from the request and say so. For a redesign, an audit should exist (`.scratch/ui-audit/<screen>/audit.md`); without one, offer `/ui-audit <screen>` first, and go on without it only on the owner's word.

Postflight: the path to `docs/ui/<screen>.md` and its captures, the chosen direction and why, the critic's verdict, the open decisions and every `> Assumed:`, and the next step: `/tickets` or `/design`.
